One summary that toggles one collapsible panel — the single primitive. An accordion is a set of these with one open at a time, which is the consumer's policy to enforce, not the component's. It drives the panel with a grid-template-rows animation (not native <details>, which can't animate), and wires the disclosure ARIA by hand. Colour the summary through --fold-disclosure-summary-bg for a call-to-open.

What is a design token?

A named role (page background, primary) that points at a raw value, so a theme re-points the role without a component knowing any colour.

Why signals-first?

Inputs and state are signals, forms are Signal Forms — nothing subscribes, nothing leaks, and the app is zoneless.

Is it accessible?

The summary is a real button with aria-expanded, pointing at the panel region via aria-controls; reduced-motion snaps instead of sliding.
