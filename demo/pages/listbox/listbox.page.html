fold-listbox — a styleable single-select built on fold-popover. The popover gives it the native top layer, flip/shift positioning, outside-click + Escape dismissal and focus return; on top sits the ARIA select pattern — role="listbox" with aria-activedescendant, full keyboard (↑/↓, Home/End, type-ahead, Enter) — and Signal-Forms wiring. Reach for it when options need custom rendering the native <select> popup can't give; otherwise fold-select stays the lighter default.
