The layout-owned side-panel region — a fold-panel-host placed once in the shell, driven imperatively by FoldPanelHostService.open(). A panel owns its own header/body/footer; the host provides the sliding shell, focus, and (for modal panels) the barrier. It's a layout concern, not a catalogued element — you never write <fold-panel>.

Clicking the button below increments a counter. Under a non-modal panel it still works and the click does not close the panel; under a modal one the background is inert and an outside click dismisses.

  • modal (default true) — freezes page scroll, inerts the background, traps focus, and a backdrop click dismisses. modal: false makes it non-modal: the page keeps scrolling & stays interactive, focus isn't trapped, and an outside click no longer closes it (header / Esc / close() do).
