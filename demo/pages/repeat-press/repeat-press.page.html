Press-and-hold auto-repeat for a bare <button>. Fires (foldRepeatPress) once immediately on press, then keeps firing on a fixed cadence while held — the stepper behaviour, without a timer in every component. Stops on release, leave, cancel, destroy, or the instant [foldRepeatPressDisabled] goes true mid-hold.
