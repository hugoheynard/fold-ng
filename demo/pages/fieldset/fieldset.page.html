fold-fieldset — a named group of form controls. A real <fieldset> / <legend> pair with its browser styling neutralised and its rhythm on the token scale. Reach for it whenever several controls answer one question: the legend names the group to a screen reader, which a heading above a <div> would look identical to and announce nothing of.
