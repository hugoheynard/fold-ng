One directive on a real <button> or <a>, driven by two orthogonal axes — emphasis (how loud) × intent (what it means). Every combination is expressible, from a quiet outline to a filled destructive CTA, and the host stays a native control so type, disabled, href and form submit come for free.

foldButton is an attribute-selector component (button[foldButton], a[foldButton]) — the host is the real button or anchor, the way Angular Material ships matButton. That's why a "link that looks like a button" needs no second component.
