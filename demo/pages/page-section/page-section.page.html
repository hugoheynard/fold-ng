collapsible folds the body and nothing else: the title, its subtitle, its description and its actions stay put. That line is the difference between folding and hiding — a tab hid the section's state along with its fields, so you could not tell what was missing without opening every one. Folded, a section still says what it is and what it is doing; the actions even stay clickable.

stack — lay the body out as evenly-spaced fields on the section gap (a form); off, the body is free-flow content that keeps its own spacing. bleed — break the section out of the page gutter to span edge-to-edge; watch it reach past the two padded siblings (it cancels --fold-page-gutter exactly).
