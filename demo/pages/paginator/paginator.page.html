fold-paginator — presentational and fully controlled, for server-side pagination: the parent owns currentPage / pageSize / totalItems, fires the fetch on (pageChange) / (pageSizeChange), and feeds the new values back. It tolerates a lagging or out-of-range page (the range + active button clamp internally), keeps keyboard focus after a page change, and every label is overridable for i18n (provideFoldPaginatorLabels, English by default).
